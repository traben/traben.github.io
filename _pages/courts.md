I'm interested in the US court system. Lots of courts live stream oral arguments and all courts publish opinions.

## Oral argument calendar

I keep an [oral argument calendar](/courts/oral-arguments/) of upcoming arguments at the Supreme Court, with a short overview of each case, why it matters, and links to listen live or to the recording. It also collects recent recordings from the federal courts of appeals. It updates itself daily.
