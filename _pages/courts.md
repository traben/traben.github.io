I'm interested in the US court system. Lots of courts live stream oral arguments and all courts publish opinions.

## Oral argument calendar

I keep an [oral argument calendar](/courts/oral-arguments/) of upcoming arguments at the Supreme Court, with a short overview of each case, why it matters, and links to listen live or to the recording. It also lists upcoming arguments from several federal courts of appeals, and collects their recent recordings. It updates itself daily.

*The plain-English case summaries on the calendar are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, from the linked sources, and may contain errors. The courts' own documents are the authoritative record.*
