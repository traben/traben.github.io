I'm interested in the US court system. Lots of courts live stream oral arguments and all courts publish opinions.

## Oral argument calendar

I keep an [oral argument calendar](/courts/oral-arguments/) that updates itself daily:

- **[Supreme Court](/courts/oral-arguments/#scotus):** every scheduled argument, with a short overview of the case, why it matters, and links to listen live or to the recording.
- **[Federal courts of appeals](/courts/oral-arguments/#circuits):** upcoming arguments from eleven of the thirteen circuits, where to listen to each court live (many stream on YouTube), and recent recordings.
- **[A few district court cases](/courts/oral-arguments/#district):** hand-picked trial-court cases I'm following, with the next hearing, how to listen in, upcoming deadlines, and a case history.

*The plain-English case summaries on the calendar are written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, from the linked sources, and may contain errors. The courts' own documents are the authoritative record.*

## Following litigation over the Trump administration

Hundreds of lawsuits have challenged actions of the second Trump administration. These trackers, all free to read except the *New York Times*, follow them from different angles.

**Case trackers**

- [**Lawfare: Trump Administration Litigation Tracker**](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/projects-series/trials-of-the-trump-administration/tracking-trump-administration-litigation): a sortable table of challenges to executive actions on national security issues and the administration's own suits to enforce them, with each case's status and a summary. It includes separate trackers for the Alien Enemies Act, government non-compliance in habeas cases, and domestic troop deployments. New filings are posted on Bluesky at [@trumplitigation.bots.law](https://bsky.app/profile/trumplitigation.bots.law), made with the Free Law Project.
- [**Just Security: Litigation Tracker**](https://www.justsecurity.org/107087/tracker-litigation-legal-challenges-trump-administration/): aims to be the most complete public record of court challenges to executive actions since Jan. 20, 2025, with running totals by status ("blocked," "on appeal," "awaiting ruling") and filters. Updated every weekday.
- [**Civil Rights Litigation Clearinghouse: Trump Administration 2.0**](https://clearinghouse.net/collections/38759): more than a thousand cases with dockets, plain-language summaries and outcomes, grouped by issue. Run by the University of Michigan Law School; good for finding the underlying court documents.
- [**New York Times: Tracking the Lawsuits Against Trump's Agenda**](https://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2025/us/trump-administration-lawsuits.html): a readable overview organized by topic (immigration, funding cuts, firings, tariffs and more). Subscription required.

**Hearing-by-hearing coverage**

- [**Lawfare: The Trials of the Trump Administration**](https://www.lawfaremedia.org/projects-series/litigation-coverage/trump-administration-trial-coverage): reporters Anna Bower, Molly Roberts and Roger Parloff write live "hearing diaries" and dispatches from courtrooms. These are accounts of what happened at a hearing and what the judge seemed to be weighing, not a list of cases. Lawfare also discusses the week's litigation on its *Lawfare Daily* podcast.

**The Supreme Court's emergency docket**

- [**Brennan Center: Supreme Court Shadow Docket Tracker**](https://www.brennancenter.org/our-work/research-reports/supreme-court-shadow-docket-tracker-challenges-trump-administration): every emergency-docket ruling on a Trump administration action, with who won, whether the Court explained itself, and the case's path through the lower courts.
- [**SCOTUSblog: Interim (emergency) docket**](https://www.scotusblog.com/case-files/emergency/emergency-docket-2025/): every emergency application this term, with filings and orders.

**Trackers on one subject**

- [Just Security: 2026 Midterms Election Integrity Litigation Tracker](https://www.justsecurity.org/162830/2026-midterms-election-integrity-litigation-tracker/)
- [Brookings: lawsuits over the administration's K-12 education agenda](https://www.brookings.edu/articles/tracking-lawsuits-challenging-the-trump-administrations-k-12-education-agenda/)
- [NAACP Legal Defense Fund: LDF's lawsuits against the administration](https://www.naacpldf.org/ldf-trump-lawsuit-tracker/) (cases LDF itself brought)

**Primary sources**

- [CourtListener](https://www.courtlistener.com/), from the Free Law Project: free federal dockets and filings (from the RECAP archive), opinions, and appellate oral argument recordings. You can set free alerts on any docket.
