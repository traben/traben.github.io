NOTICE TO ALL COUNSEL 
 
Please review the entire calendar, as you may have more than one case scheduled 
for oral argument. Please check the calendar carefully for the starting time for your day 
and division. 
 
Court will be held in the Thomas F. Eagleton Courthouse at 111 S. 10 th Street, St. 
Louis, Missouri. Division I will be held in the En Banc Courtroom on Floor 28.  Counsel 
presenting oral argument should report to the c ourtroom, 30 minutes before Court 
convenes.   
 
Counsel may not waive oral argument without prior leave of court.  Absent leave 
of court, only one attorney is permitted to present the argument for a party. However, if 
the parties on a side are separately represented and have filed separate briefs, each party 
on that side is permitted to argue; in such a case, counsel must confer and decide how 
they will split the time shown on the calendar.  Questions should be addressed to the 
Clerk of Court. 
 
Counsel are required to file an AArgument Response/Appearance@ form with the 
Clerk=s Office identifying the party or parties they will be representing at oral argument.  
If an A Appearance@ form has not already been filed, the A Argument 
Response/Appearance@ form serves as your notice of appearance in the case.  Every 
attorney who presents oral argument must be admitted to the Eighth Circuit Bar unless 
the attorney has been appointed to represent a party proceeding in forma pauperis or 
represents the United States. 
 
The time allotted for each side=s argument is noted to the right of the case.  Cases 
designated as NO ARG will be submitted to the Court on the briefs and record without 
oral argument, and counsel are not required to appear for cases designated NO ARG.
