NOTICE TO ALL COUNSEL 
 
Please review the entire calendar, as you may have more than one case 
scheduled for oral argument. Please check the calendar carefully for the starting 
time for your day and division. 
 
Court will be held in the Warren E. Burger Federal Building & U.S. 
Courthouse at 316 N. Robert Street, St. Paul, Minnesota. Counsel presenting oral 
argument should report to the Clerk ’s Office, Room 500, 30 minutes before Court 
convenes. 
 
Counsel may not waive oral argument without prior leave of court. Absent 
leave of court, only one attorney is permitted to present the argument for a party. 
However, if the parties on a side are separately represented and have filed separate 
briefs, each party on that side is permitted to argue; in such a case, counsel must 
confer and decide how they will split the time shown on the calendar. Questions 
should be addressed to the Clerk of Court. 
 
Counsel are required to file an “ Oral Argument Response/Appearance” 
form with the Clerk’s Office identifying the party or parties they will be 
representing at oral argument. If an “Appearance” form has not already been filed, 
the “ Oral Argument Response/Appearance” form serves as your notice of 
appearance in the case.  Every attorney who presents oral argument must be 
admitted to the Eighth Circuit Bar unless the attorney has been appointed to 
represent a party proceeding in forma pauperis or represents the United States. 
 
The time allotted for each side ’s argument is noted to the right of the case.  
Cases designated as NO ARG will be submitted to the Court on the briefs and 
record without oral argument, and counsel are not required to appear for cases 
designated NO ARG.
